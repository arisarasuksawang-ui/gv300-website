## About this site

This website was created as part of the GV300 Data Analysis module. It presents examples of my work using R to explore and visualise political and social data.

The aim of the site is to demonstrate how data analysis can be used to communicate ideas clearly and effectively.

## About me

I am a Politics and International Relations student interested in political behaviour and data analysis. I use R to analyse datasets and produce visualisations that help explain patterns in political and social data.

## What you can find on this site
- **Home page** – introduction to the project and a visualisation of UK constituency sizes.  
- **Profile page** – examples of my work, including a data visualisation project analysing mothers' ages in the dataset.
